# Code of Conduct

Nimbus is an open source project for people who want better game streaming tools.
Project spaces should stay useful, respectful, and focused on the work.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep feedback specific, technical, and actionable.
- Help new contributors understand the project without gatekeeping.
- Accept maintainer decisions once a direction is chosen.
- Disclose conflicts of interest when they matter to a discussion.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussions.
- Pressuring maintainers for unpaid real-time support.
- Posting secrets, exploit details, or private logs in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments and issues that violate this
code. Maintainers may also temporarily or permanently block users from project
spaces when behavior harms the community or the project.

If you need to report a conduct concern, contact a maintainer through the least
public channel available for the situation.
